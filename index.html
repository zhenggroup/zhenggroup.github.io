Electrochemical Interface & Conversion Lab

AT THE
INTERFACE
OF DISCOVERY.

Led by Dr. Weiran Zheng, we study electrochemical interfaces in real time with operando spectroscopy, scanning probe microscopy, and data-driven electrochemical methods. Our goal is to reveal how catalytic activity, selectivity, and degradation evolve under working conditions, enabling more efficient and durable electrocatalysis.
