Electrochemical Interface & Conversion Lab

AT THE
INTERFACE
OF DISCOVERY.

Led by Dr. Weiran Zheng, we study how electrochemical interfaces evolve during water electrolysis and ammonia oxidation. We combine operando spectroscopy, electrochemical microscopy, and time-resolved impedance analysis to connect surface changes with activity, selectivity, and degradation.

We also develop quantitative methods, including dynamic iR correction, for reliable evaluation of catalysts and electrode materials.
